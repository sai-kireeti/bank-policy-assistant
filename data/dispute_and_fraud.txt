Dispute and Fraud Policy

If a customer does not know a charge on the card, the agent should treat it as a possible fraud case. The agent should block the card at once if the customer confirms the charge is not theirs.

A customer can raise a dispute within 60 days from the statement date of the charge. The agent should note the date, the amount, and the merchant name for every dispute.

After a dispute is raised, the bank will check the case and may give a temporary credit within 10 business days. The final answer is shared within 45 days in most cases.

If the charge is a known charge but the product was not received, it is not fraud. The agent should raise it as a service dispute and ask the customer for proof of purchase or delivery messages.

The agent should never ask the customer to share the full card number, PIN, or one time password on a call or in chat. The agent should only ask for the last four digits of the card.
