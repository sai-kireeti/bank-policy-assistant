Account Opening Policy

To open a new account, a customer must be 18 years or older. The customer must share a valid photo ID, such as a passport or a driving license.

The customer must also share proof of address, such as a utility bill or a bank statement from the last 90 days. The name on the ID and the name on the proof of address should match.

The first deposit can be made by debit card, by bank transfer, or by check. There is no minimum first deposit for a basic checking account in this sample policy.

If a document is not clear or is expired, the agent should ask the customer to share a new copy. The agent should not open the account until all documents are clear and valid.

After the account is open, the customer gets a welcome email within one business day. The debit card is sent by mail and should arrive in 5 to 7 business days.
